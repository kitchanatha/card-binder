Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

In short: Card Binder has no accounts and keeps no database of users.
  • Your collection is saved only in your own browser.
  • If you turn on Google Drive sync, your collection is saved as one file in your Google Drive. Card Binder can see only that file.
  • We don't sell or share personal data and there are no ads. Visits are counted with cookieless Vercel Web Analytics, which doesn't identify you.

Who runs Card Binder

Card Binder (card-binder-xi.vercel.app) is a free, non-commercial fan project for browsing Pokémon and ONE PIECE trading cards. It is not affiliated with The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Bandai or Shueisha.

Your collection

The cards you mark as owned, your language choice and similar settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so use Export file in My collection to keep a backup. Exported files stay wherever you save them.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive, Google asks for your permission and gives Card Binder access with the drive.file scope. This only allows Card Binder to create, read and update files it created itself; it cannot see any other file in your Drive.

  • Card Binder creates one file, card-binder-collection.json, containing the IDs of the cards you own and when you marked them.
  • The sign-in happens in your browser. The access token stays in your browser's memory, expires after about an hour, and is never sent to Card Binder's server.
  • Card Binder does not receive your name, email address, contacts or any other Google account data.
  • You can disconnect at any time with Disconnect in My collection, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the file from your Drive deletes the synced copy.

Card Binder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information sent to other services

To show cards, images and prices, your browser loads data directly from these services, which see the usual technical information any website receives (such as your IP address and browser type) under their own privacy policies:

  • Card data and images: TCGdex, pokemontcg.io, GitHub (punk-records data), optcgapi, TCGplayer's image server, and the official ONE PIECE Card Game site (through our image server).
  • PSA 10 prices: when you open a Pokémon card, our server asks Pokémon Price Tracker for that card's prices. Only the card's name, number and set are sent, nothing about you.
  • Hosting: the site runs on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs for security and reliability.

Visit statistics

Card Binder uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It uses no cookies and doesn't identify you or follow you across sites. It records only totals such as page views, the page you came from, country, browser, operating system and device type. These numbers help decide what to improve.

Links to other sites

Support on Ko-fi, Request a feature and Report a bug open Ko-fi and GitHub. Anything you post there, such as a bug report on GitHub, is public and handled under those sites' own policies.

Children

Card Binder collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. For questions, open an issue at github.com/kitchanatha/card-binder/issues or use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen.
